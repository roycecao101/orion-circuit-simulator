Experiment guide

In series, every load carries the same current and shares the available voltage. In parallel, loads share a voltage and their currents add at the supply.

Try opening one load in each topology. Then raise wire resistance and watch the terminal voltage, load voltage, and efficiency change.

ONLINE ≥85% of target · LIMITED ≥55% · CRITICAL <55%. Targets are teaching thresholds, not device ratings. Shorted loads receive zero voltage.

Physics model: An ideal DC voltage source has series internal resistance and shared supply/return wire resistance. Loads are ideal resistors in a series chain or parallel branches. Open faults have infinite resistance; short faults have zero resistance. This editor supports these two network layouts, not arbitrary wiring. Animated markers illustrate flow direction and relative current, not physical electron speed. A zero-resistance path with an ideal source is reported as an ideal short circuit.
References: Halliday, Resnick & Walker, Fundamentals of Physics, 12th ed. (2021); Serway & Jewett, Physics for Scientists and Engineers, 10th ed. (2019). DC circuits, Ohm’s law, internal resistance, and electrical power.
